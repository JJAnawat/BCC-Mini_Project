# DEAP EDA Summary — Week 1

Files named below are in `outputs/eda/`. Code: `pipeline/eda/`.

## 1. Structural integrity
All 32 subjects: `data` shape `(40, 40, 8064)`, `labels` shape `(40, 4)`, no NaNs, no all-zero trials, 1280/1280 trials present. Subjects 3, 5, 11, 14 (known face-video recording gap) confirmed EEG-unaffected: 40/40 trials each.

## 2. Label bug found and fixed
`.dat` files' `labels[:,0:2]` (valence, arousal) are corrupted on **439/1280 trials (34%)**: for those trials `dat_value == 9 - true_value` exactly, and `.dat` valence/arousal fall outside the documented 1–9 range (down to 0.0) while `Metadata/participant_ratings.xls` never does. Dominance/Liking match `.dat` exactly on every trial — only valence/arousal needed replacing. **Decision: all downstream label consumption uses `participant_ratings.xls`, joined by `Participant_id`+`Experiment_id`.**

## 3. Class balance
- Pooled binarized (≥5 = high): **56.6% high valence, 58.9% high arousal** — roughly balanced; accuracy is defensible, F1/AUC still recommended as a secondary metric.
- **Quadrant counts (the number that actually matters for quadrant classification):**

  | Quadrant | Count | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | HVHA | 458 | 35.8% |
  | LVHA | 296 | 23.1% |
  | HVLA | 266 | 20.8% |
  | LVLA | 260 | 20.3% |

  Max/min ratio **1.76x** — moderate imbalance. Use class-weighted loss or macro-F1 for quadrant classification; full resampling not obviously required.
- **Subject 23 has zero LVHA trials.** In LOSO CV, the fold holding out subject 23 cannot evaluate LVHA — handle explicitly in the eval script (report separately / skip), don't silently average a NaN into overall metrics.
- 0 subjects show >90/10 label skew on valence or arousal after correction.

## 4. Signal quality
- No flat/dead channels anywhere.
- 16 (subject, channel) pairs flagged as amplitude outliers (std > 3 SD within-subject) across 14 subjects — informational only, see `task3_amplitude_stats.csv`. **Decision: no channels are excluded; all 32 channels are kept for every subject.**
- **Subject 11 inspected directly** (`task3_psd_s11.png`, `task3_waveform_s11_trial0.png`): F7 spikes to ~1000µV, 5–10x the simultaneous amplitude on Fp1/Fp2/Oz/Cz at the same timestamps — an electrode-level artifact localized to F7. PSD confirms F7 dominates broadband power and subject 11 shows no clean physiological alpha peak (contrast against subject 1's clean alpha bump). **Decision: keep subject 11 and channel F7** — noted for awareness, no exclusion applied.
- Subject 22 is an alpha-power outlier with no channel-level cause found — kept as-is, flagged for monitoring after feature extraction.

## 5. Locked config
All decisions above are frozen in `config.yaml` at the project root (loaded/validated via `src/bcc/config.py`): label source, binarization threshold, quadrant balance reference numbers, the 32-channel order + 14 symmetric pairs (no channels excluded), 4s non-overlapping windowing (15 windows/trial, exact — no partial window), and theta/alpha/beta/gamma band definitions. Downstream scripts must import from this file rather than redefining any of these values.

Supplementary evidence: `compare_s01_s11_s22_psd.png` / `compare_s01_s11_s22_waveform.png` (shared y-axis across subjects 1/11/22, generated by Task 3) corroborate the subject 11 decision above.
